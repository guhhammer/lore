# cupsdisable

Stop printers and classes. Note: Destination is referred as a printer or a class of printers.

## Usage
- `cupsdisable destination1 destination2 ...` : stop one or more destination(s)
- `cupsdisable -c destination1 destination2 ...` : cancel all jobs of the specified destination(s)
