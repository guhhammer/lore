# udiskie-dmenu

A dmenu/rofi-based frontend for udiskie to mount and unmount devices interactively. Requires udiskie and either dmenu or rofi.

## Usage
- `udiskie-dmenu` : launch the menu to mount or unmount devices
- `udiskie-dmenu --rofi` : use rofi instead of dmenu
- `udiskie-dmenu --help` : display help
