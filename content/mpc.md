# mpc

Music Player Client: control the Music Player Daemon (MPD).

## Usage
- `mpc toggle` : toggle play/pause
- `mpc stop` : stop playing
- `mpc status` : show information about the currently playing song
- `mpc next` : play the next song
- `mpc prev` : play the previous song
- `mpc +n|-n` : seek `n` seconds forward (`+`) or backward (`-`)
