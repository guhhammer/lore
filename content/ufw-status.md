# ufw status

Show the status of Uncomplicated Firewall and its rules.

## Usage
- `sudo ufw status` : show whether the firewall is active and list the rules
- `sudo ufw status numbered` : show rules with numbers (useful before deleting a rule by number)
- `sudo ufw status verbose` : show a verbose status, including default policies and listening ports
