# pwgen

Generate pronounceable passwords.

## Usage
- `pwgen` : generate 160 random passwords with character length of 8
- `pwgen 20` : generate passwords with character length of 20
- `pwgen 20 10` : generate 10 passwords with character length of 20
- `pwgen -y` : generate random passwords with symbols
- `pwgen -s` : generate secure, hard-to-memorize passwords
- `pwgen -c length count` : generate password with at least one capital letter in them, specify their length, and how many to generate
