# tred

Compute the transitive reduction of directed graphs. Graphviz filters: `acyclic`, `bcomps`, `comps`, `edgepaint`, `gvcolor`, `gvpack`, `mingle`, `nop`, `sccmap`, `tred`, and `unflatten`.

## Usage
- `tred path/to/input1.gv path/to/input2.gv ... >path/to/output.gv` : construct the transitive reduction graph of one or more directed graphs
- `tred -?` : display help
