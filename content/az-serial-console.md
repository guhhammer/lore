# az serial-console

Connect to the serial console of a Virtual Machine. Part of `azure-cli` (also known as `az`).

## Usage
- `az serial-console connect -g Resource_Group_Name -n Virtual_Machine_Name` : connect to a serial console
- `<Ctrl ]>` : terminate the connection
