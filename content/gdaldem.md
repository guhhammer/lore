# gdaldem

Analyze and visualize digital elevation models (DEM).

## Usage
- `gdaldem hillshade path/to/input.tif path/to/output.tif` : compute the hillshade of a DEM
- `gdaldem slope path/to/input.tif path/to/output.tif` : compute the slope of a DEM
- `gdaldem aspect path/to/input.tif path/to/output.tif` : compute the aspect of a DEM
