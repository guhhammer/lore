- `hmm-assembler.pl -i` : <length>       [500]
- `hmm-assembler.pl -e` : <length>       [1000]
- `hmm-assembler.pl -A` : <order:length> [0:30]
- `hmm-assembler.pl -D` : <order:length> [0:9]
