# ppmshift

Shift the lines in a PPM image by a randomized amount.

## Usage
- `ppmshift s path/to/input_file.ppm >path/to/output_file.ppm` : shift the lines in the input image by a randomized amount not exceeding s to the left or to the right

## Categories
containers, media, text
