# mssh

GTK+ based SSH client for interacting with multiple SSH servers at once.

## Usage
- `mssh user@host1 user@host2 ...` : open a new window and connect to multiple SSH servers
- `mssh -a alias_name` : open a new window and connect to a group of servers predefined in `~/.mssh_clusters`
