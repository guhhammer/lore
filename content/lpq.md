# lpq

Show printer queue status.

## Usage
- `lpq` : show the queued jobs of the default destination
- `lpq -a -E` : show the queued jobs of all printers enforcing encryption
- `lpq -l` : show the queued jobs in a long format
- `lpq -P destination/instance` : show the queued jobs of a specific printer or class
- `lpq +interval` : show the queued jobs once every n seconds until the queue is empty
