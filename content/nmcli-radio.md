# nmcli radio

Show the status of radio switches or enable/disable them using NetworkManager.

## Usage
- `nmcli r w` : show status of Wi-Fi
- `nmcli r w on|off` : turn Wi-Fi on or off
- `nmcli r ww` : show status of WWAN
- `nmcli r ww on|off` : turn WWAN on or off
- `nmcli r` : show status of both switches
- `nmcli r a on|off` : turn both switches on or off
