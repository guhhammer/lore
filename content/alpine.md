# alpine

An email client and Usenet newsgroup program with a pico/nano-inspired interface. Supports most modern email services through IMAP.

## Usage
- `alpine` : open alpine normally
- `alpine email@example.net` : open alpine directly to the message composition screen to send an email to a given email address
- `<q><y>` : quit alpine
