# dbus-uuidgen

Utility to generate UUIDs.

## Usage
- `dbus-uuidgen [--version] [--ensure [=FILENAME]] [--get [=FILENAME]]` : the general form, as the manual gives it
- `dbus-uuidgen --version` : print the version of dbus-uuidgen
