# mm2gv

Convert a graph from Matrix Market `mm` format to `gv` format. Converters: `gml2gv`, `gv2gml`, `gv2gxl`, `gxl2gv`, `graphml2gv` & `mm2gv`.

## Usage
- `mm2gv -o output.gv input.mm` : convert a graph from `mm` to `gv` format
- `cat input.mm | mm2gv >output.gv` : convert a graph using `stdin` and `stdout`
- `mm2gv -?` : display help
