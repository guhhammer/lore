# pgmenhance

Edge-enhance a PGM image.

## Usage
- `pgmenhance path/to/image.pgm >path/to/output.pgm` : edge-enhance a PGM image
- `pgmenhance -1..9 path/to/image.pgm >path/to/output.pgm` : specify the level of enhancement
