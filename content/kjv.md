# kjv

The word of God available right on your desktop.

## Usage
- `kjv -l` : display books
- `kjv Genesis` : open a specific book
- `kjv Genesis 2` : open a specific chapter of a book
- `kjv John 3:16` : open a specific verse of a specific chapter of a book
- `kjv Proverbs 3:1-6` : open a specific range of verses of a book's chapter
- `kjv Matthew 1:7-2:6` : display a specific range of verses of a book from different chapters
- `kjv /Plagues` : display all verses that match a pattern
- `kjv 1Jn/antichrist` : display all verses that match a pattern in a specific book
