## Usage
- `totem [option...] [filenames|URI]` : the general form, as the manual gives it
- `totem --version` : output version information then quit
- `totem --volume-up` : tell an already-running instance of Videos to raise the volume by 8%
- `totem --volume-down` : tell an already-running instance of Videos to lower the volume by 8%
- `totem --fullscreen` : tell an already-running instance of Videos to toggle fullscreen mode
