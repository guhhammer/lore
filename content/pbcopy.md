# pbcopy

Copy data from `stdin` to the clipboard. Comparable to pressing `<Cmd c>` on the keyboard.

## Usage
- `pbcopy <path/to/file` : place the contents of a specific file in the clipboard
- `find . -type t -name "*.png" | pbcopy` : place the results of a specific command in the clipboard
