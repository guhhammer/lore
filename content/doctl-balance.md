# doctl balance

Show the balance of a Digital Ocean account.

## Usage
- `doctl balance g` : get balance of the account associated with the current context
- `doctl balance g -t access_token` : get the balance of an account associated with an access token
- `doctl balance g --context` : get the balance of an account associated with a specified context
