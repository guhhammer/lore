# ssimulacra2

Score how visually similar a distorted image is to its original. Scores range from negative (very strong distortion) through 90 (visually lossless) to 100 (mathematically lossless).

## Usage
- `ssimulacra2 path/to/original.png path/to/distorted.png` : score a distorted image against the original (both images must have the same dimensions)
- `ssimulacra2` : display the usage and what each score range means
