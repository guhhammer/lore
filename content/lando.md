# lando

Local development environment and DevOps tool built on Docker.

## Usage
- `lando init` : initialize code for use with `lando`
- `lando info` : print information about your app
- `lando start` : start your app
- `lando stop` : stop your app
- `lando restart` : restart your app
- `lando rebuild` : rebuild your app from scratch, preserving data
- `lando logs` : display logs for your app
- `lando destroy` : destroy your app
