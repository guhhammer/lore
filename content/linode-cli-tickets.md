# linode-cli tickets

Manage Linode Support Tickets.

## Usage
- `linode-cli tickets list` : list your Support Tickets
- `linode-cli tickets create --summary "Summary or quick title for the Ticket" --description "Detailed description of the issue"` : open a new Ticket
- `linode-cli tickets replies ticket_id` : list replies to a Ticket
- `linode-cli tickets reply ticket_id --description "The content of your reply"` : reply to a specific Ticket
