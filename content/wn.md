# wn

Look up related words in a WordNet database.

## Usage
- `wn emphatically` : show information available for the word "emphatically"
- `wn emphatically -synsr` : list synonyms of the adverb "emphatically"
- `wn slow -antsa` : list antonyms of the adjective "slow"
