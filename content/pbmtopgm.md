# pbmtopgm

Convert a PBM image to PGM by averaging areas surrounding individual pixels.

## Usage
- `pbmtopgm w h path/to/image.pbm >path/to/output.pgm` : convert PBM image to PGM by averaging the `w`x`h`-sized area surrounding each pixel
