# lowntfs-3g

Third Generation Read/Write NTFS Driver.

## Usage
- `ntfs-3g [-o option[,...]] volume mount_point mount -t ntfs-3g [-o option[,...]] volume mount_point` : the general form, as the manual gives it
- `lowntfs-3g [-o option[,...]] volume mount_point mount -t lowntfs-3g [-o option[,...]] volume mount_point` : another form of the same command
