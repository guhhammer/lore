# plymouthd

The plymouth daemon.

## Usage
- `plymouthd [OPTION...]` : the general form, as the manual gives it
- `plymouthd --help` : show summary of options
- `plymouthd --attach-to-session` : redirect console messages from screen to log
- `plymouthd --no-daemon` : do not daemonize
- `plymouthd --debug` : output debugging information
