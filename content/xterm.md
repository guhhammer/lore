# xterm

A terminal emulator for the X Window System.

## Usage
- `xterm -T Example` : open the terminal with a title of `Example`
- `xterm -fullscreen` : open the terminal in fullscreen mode
- `xterm -bg darkblue -fg yellow` : open the terminal with a dark blue background and yellow foreground (font color)
- `xterm -geometry 100x35+200+20` : open the terminal with 100 characters per line and 35 lines, in screen position x=200px, y=20px
- `xterm -fa 'Serif' -fs 20` : open the terminal using a Serif font and a font size equal to 20
