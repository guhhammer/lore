# rpicam-raw

Capture a raw video on a Raspberry Pi camera.

## Usage
- `rpicam-raw -t 2000 -o path/to/file.raw` : capture a video for a specific amount of seconds
- `rpicam-raw -t 5000 --width 4056 --height 3040 -o path/to/file.raw --framerate 8` : change video dimensions and framerate
