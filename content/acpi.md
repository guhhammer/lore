# acpi

Show battery status or thermal information.

## Usage
- `acpi` : show battery information
- `acpi -t` : show thermal information
- `acpi -c` : show cooling device information
- `acpi -tf` : show thermal information in Fahrenheit
- `acpi -V` : show all information
- `acpi -p` : extract information from `/proc` instead of `/sys`
