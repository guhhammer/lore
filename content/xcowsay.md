# xcowsay

Display a cute cow and message on your Linux desktop. The cow is displayed for either a fixed amount of time, or an amount of time calculated from the size of the text. Click on the cow to dismiss it immediately.

## Usage
- `xcowsay "hello, world"` : display a cow saying "hello, world"
- `ls | xcowsay` : display a cow with output from another command
- `xcowsay --at X,Y` : display a cow at the specified X and Y coordinates
- `xcowsay --cow-size small|med|large` : display a different sized cow
- `xcowsay --think` : display a thought bubble instead of a speech bubble
- `xcowsay --image path/to/file` : display a different image instead of the default cow
