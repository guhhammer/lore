# treetime

Provide routines for ancestral sequence reconstruction and inference of molecular-clock phylogenies.

## Usage
- `treetime ancestral` : infer ancestral sequences maximizing the joint or marginal likelihood
- `treetime homoplasy` : analyze patterns of recurrent mutations aka homoplasies
- `treetime clock` : estimate molecular clock parameters and reroot the tree
- `treetime mugration` : map discrete character such as host or country to the tree
