# nhentai

Download doujinshis from nhentai.

## Usage
- `nhentai --cookie "csrftoken=token; sessionid=session_id"` : set cookies
- `nhentai --id number` : download a specific doujin
- `nhentai -F -D -d 1` : download the first page of your favorites
- `nhentai -F --pages start_page-end_page -D -d 1` : download specific pages of your favorites
