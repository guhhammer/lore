# pnmmercator

Perform Mercator transformations on Netpbm images.

## Usage
- `pnmmercator path/to/image.pnm >path/to/output.pnm` : convert a rectangular projection worldmap to Mercator projection
- `pnmmercator -i path/to/image.pnm >path/to/output.pnm` : convert a Mercator projection worldmap to rectangular projection
