# openclaw channels

Manage OpenClaw channels (WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, etc.).

## Usage
- `openclaw channels list` : list all configured channels
- `openclaw channels login --channel whatsapp` : log in to a channel
- `openclaw channels login --channel telegram --verbose` : log in to a specific channel with verbose logging
- `openclaw channels -h` : display help
