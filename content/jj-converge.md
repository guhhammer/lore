# jj converge

Converge divergent changes. Attempts to resolve divergence by replacing two or more visible revisions for a given change with a single revision.

## Usage
- `jj converge` : converge divergent revisions in the default search space (configured by `revsets.converge`)
- `jj converge -r revset` : converge divergent revisions within a specific search space
- `jj converge --no-interactive` : resolve divergence without interactive prompts
