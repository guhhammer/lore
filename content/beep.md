# beep

A utility to beep the PC speaker.

## Usage
- `beep` : play a beep
- `beep -r repetitions` : play a beep that repeats
- `beep -f frequency -l duration` : play a beep at a specified frequency (Hz) and duration (milliseconds)
- `beep -f frequency -l duration -n -f frequency -l duration` : play each new frequency and duration as a distinct beep
- `beep -f 262 -n -f 294 -n -f 330 -n -f 349 -n -f 392 -n -f 440 -n -f 494 -n -f 523` : play the C major scale
