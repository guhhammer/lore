# conjure-im7

Interprets and executes scripts written in the Magick Scripting Language (MSL).

## Usage
- `magick-im7.q16 conjure [options] script.msl` : the general form, as the manual gives it
- `conjure-im7 -monitor` : monitor progress
- `conjure-im7 -quiet` : suppress all warning messages
- `conjure-im7 -regard-warnings` : pay attention to warning messages
- `conjure-im7 -seed value` : seed a new sequence of pseudo-random numbers
