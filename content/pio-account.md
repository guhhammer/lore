# pio account

Manage your PlatformIO account.

## Usage
- `pio account register -u username -e email -p password --firstname firstname --lastname lastname` : register a new PlatformIO account
- `pio account destroy` : permanently delete your PlatformIO account and related data
- `pio account login -u username -p password` : log in to your PlatformIO account
- `pio account logout` : log out of your PlatformIO account
- `pio account update -u username -e email --firstname firstname --lastname lastname --current-password password` : update your PlatformIO profile
- `pio account show` : show detailed information about your PlatformIO account
- `pio account forgot -u username_or_email` : reset your password using your username or email
