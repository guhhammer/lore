# rgb3toppm

Combine three PGM images into one PPM image.

## Usage
- `rgb3toppm path/to/red_file.ppm path/to/green_file.ppm path/to/blue_file.ppm >path/to/output.ppm` : combine three PGM images (representing red, green, and blue color components) into one PPM image
