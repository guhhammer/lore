# pdfposter

Convert a large-sheeted PDF into multiple A4 pages for printing.

## Usage
- `pdfposter -p a2 input_file.pdf output_file.pdf` : convert an A2 poster into 4 A4 pages
- `pdfposter -s 2 input_file.pdf output_file.pdf` : scale an A4 poster to A3 and then generate 2 A4 pages
