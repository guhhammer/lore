# i2cdetect

Scan I2C buses.

## Usage
- `i2cdetect -l` : list active I2C buses
- `i2cdetect i2c_bus` : scan devices on an I2C bus
- `i2cdetect -y i2c_bus` : scan devices on an I2C bus without asking for confirmation
