# protonvpn

Official Proton VPN client. Some subcommands such as `connect` have their own usage documentation.

## Usage
- `protonvpn signin` : initialize Proton VPN profile
- `protonvpn signout` : remove account credentials
- `protonvpn info` : display Proton VPN account information
- `protonvpn connect` : connect to Proton VPN interactively
- `protonvpn disconnect` : disconnect from Proton VPN
- `protonvpn -h` : display help
