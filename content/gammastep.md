# gammastep

Adjust the screen's color temperature according to the time of day.

## Usage
- `gammastep -t 5700:3600` : turn on Gammastep with temperature set to 5700k during the day and 3600k at night
- `gammastep -l latitude:longitude` : turn on Gammastep with a manually specified custom location
- `gammastep -b 0.7:0.4` : turn on Gammastep with screen brightness set to 70% during the day and 40% at night
- `gammastep -g red:green:blue` : turn on Gammastep with custom gamma levels (between 0 and 1)
- `gammastep -O temperature` : turn on Gammastep with a cOnstant unchanging color temperature
- `gammastep -x` : reset temperature adjustments applied by Gammastep
