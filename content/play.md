# play

Audio player of SoX - Sound eXchange. Plays any audio, with audio formats identified by the extension.

## Usage
- `play path/to/audio_file` : play the given audio file
- `play path/to/audio_file1 path/to/audio_file2 ...` : play the given audio files
- `play path/to/audio_file speed 2.0` : play the given audio at twice the speed
- `play path/to/audio_file reverse` : play the given audio in reverse
