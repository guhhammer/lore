# paper

Print paper size information.

## Usage
- `paper [OPTION...] [PAPER...|--all]` : the general form, as the manual gives it
- `paper --all` : show information about all known paper sizes
- `paper --no-size` : do not show paper sizes (width followed by height)
- `paper --unit UNIT` : unit in which to show sizes [pt, mm, in; default: natural units]
- `paper --version` : display version information and exit
