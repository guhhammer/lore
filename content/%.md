# %

Manage jobs.

## Usage
- `%` : bring the current job to front
- `%-` : bring the previous job to front
- `%n` : bring the job number `n` to front
- `%string` : bring a job whose command starts with `string` to front
- `%?string` : bring a job whose command contains `string` to front
- `%1 &` : resume a suspended job
