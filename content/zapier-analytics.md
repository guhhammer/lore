# zapier analytics

Show the status of the analytics that are collected. It is also used to change what is collected.

## Usage
- `zapier analytics` : show the status of collected analytics
- `zapier analytics -m enabled|anonymous|disabled` : change how much information is collected
- `zapier analytics -m enabled|anonymous|disabled -d` : show extra debugging output
