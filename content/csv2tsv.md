# csv2tsv

Convert CSV (comma-separated) text to TSV (tab-separated) format.

## Usage
- `csv2tsv path/to/input_csv1 path/to/input_csv2 ... >path/to/output_tsv` : convert from CSV to TSV
- `csv2tsv -c'field_delimiter' path/to/input_csv` : convert field delimiter separated CSV to TSV
- `csv2tsv -c';' path/to/input_csv` : convert semicolon separated CSV to TSV
