# terraform login

Log in to a remote host.

## Usage
- `terraform login` : log in to HCP Terraform (app.terraform.io)
- `terraform login hostname` : log in to a specific hostname
- `terraform login app.terraform.io/eu` : log in to HCP Terraform Europe

## See also
