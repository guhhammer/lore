# dvc freeze

Freeze stages in the DVC pipeline. This prevents DVC from tracking changes in stage dependencies and re-execution until unfreeze.

## Usage
- `dvc freeze stage_name1 stage_name2 ...` : freeze one or more specified stages

## See also
- dvc unfreeze
