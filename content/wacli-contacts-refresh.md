# wacli contacts refresh

Synchronize contacts from the WhatsApp store.

## Usage
- `wacli contacts refresh` : refresh all contacts from the store
- `wacli contacts refresh --store path/to/store` : refresh contacts using a custom store

## See also
- wacli contacts search
- wacli contacts show
