# rtkitctl

Realtime Policy and Watchdog daemon control.

## Usage
- `rtkitctl [options]` : the general form, as the manual gives it
- `rtkitctl -h` : show a summary of options
- `rtkitctl --version` : show version
- `rtkitctl --reset-known` : reset real-time status of known threads
- `rtkitctl --reset-all` : reset real-time status of all threads
