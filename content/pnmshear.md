# pnmshear

Shear a PNM image.

## Usage
- `pnmshear angle path/to/input.pnm >path/to/output.pnm` : shear a PNM image by the specified angle
- `pnmshear -b blue angle path/to/input.pnm >path/to/output.pnm` : specify the color of the background in the sheared image
- `pnmshear -n angle path/to/input.pnm >path/to/output.pnm` : do not perform anti-aliasing
