# slop

Get a selection of the screen.

## Usage
- `slop` : wait for the user to make a selection and output its geometry to `stdout`
- `slop -D` : double click, rather than click and drag, to draw a selection
- `slop -l` : highlight the selection rather than outlining it
- `slop -f format_string` : specify the output format
- `slop -c red,green,blue,alpha` : specify the selection rectangle's color
