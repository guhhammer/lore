# systemctl suspend-then-hibernate

Suspend the system, then automatically hibernate after a period of inactivity.

## Usage
- `systemctl suspend-then-hibernate` : suspend the system and hibernate after the configured delay
- `systemctl suspend-then-hibernate -f` : force suspend-then-hibernate (bypass inhibitor locks)
