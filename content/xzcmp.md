# xzcmp

Invoke `cmp` on files compressed with `xz`, `lzma`, `gzip`, `bzip2`, `lzop`, or `zstd`. All options specified are passed directly to `cmp`.

## Usage
- `xzcmp path/to/file1 path/to/file2` : compare two specific files
