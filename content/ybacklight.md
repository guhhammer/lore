# ybacklight

Manage screen backlight brightness.

## Usage
- `ybacklight Sc/Sm` : print current brightness and maximal brightness, shortened and separated by a slash
- `ybacklight s420` : specify the brightness
- `ybacklight Si42` : increase the brightness by 42 big steps (4200 by default)
- `ybacklight d300` : decrease the brightness by 300
