# dpkg-checkbuilddeps

Check build dependencies and conflicts.

## Usage
- `dpkg-checkbuilddeps [option...] [control-file]` : the general form, as the manual gives it
- `dpkg-checkbuilddeps -B` : ignore Build-Depends-Indep and Build-Conflicts-Indep lines
- `dpkg-checkbuilddeps --version` : show the version and exit
