# pstoedit

Convert PDF files into various image formats.

## Usage
- `pstoedit -page page_number -f magick path/to/file.pdf page.png|jpg` : convert a PDF page to PNG or JPEG format
- `pstoedit -f magick path/to/file page%d.png|jpg` : convert multiple PDF pages to numbered images
