# ppmspread

Displace the pixels in a PPM image by a randomized amount.

## Usage
- `ppmspread a path/to/input_file.ppm >path/to/output_file.ppm` : displace the pixels in a PPM image by a randomized amount that is at most `a`
- `ppmspread a path/to/input_file.ppm -r seed >path/to/output_file.ppm` : specify a seed to a the pseudo-random number generator
