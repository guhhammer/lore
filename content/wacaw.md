# wacaw

Capture both still pictures and video from an attached camera.

## Usage
- `wacaw filename` : take a picture from webcam
- `wacaw --video filename -D 10` : record a video
- `wacaw -x width -y 100 filename` : take a picture with custom resolution
- `wacaw --to-clipboard` : copy image just taken to clipboard
- `wacaw -L` : list the devices available
