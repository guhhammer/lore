# atq

Show jobs scheduled by `at` or `batch` commands.

## Usage
- `atq` : show the current user's scheduled jobs
- `atq -q a` : show jobs from the `a` queue (queues have single-character names)
- `sudo atq` : show jobs of all users (run as superuser)
