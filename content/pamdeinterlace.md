# pamdeinterlace

Remove every other row in a Netpbm image.

## Usage
- `pamdeinterlace path/to/image.ppm >path/to/output.ppm` : produce an image consisting of the input's even-numbered rows
- `pamdeinterlace -takeo path/to/image.ppm >path/to/output.ppm` : produce an image consisting of the input's odd-numbered rows
