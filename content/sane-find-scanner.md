# sane-find-scanner

Find SCSI and USB scanners and their device files.

## Usage
- `sane-find-scanner [-?|-h|--help] [-v] [-q] [-p] [-f] [-F filename] [devname]` : the general form, as the manual gives it
- `sane-find-scanner -v` : verbose  output
- `sane-find-scanner -q` : be quiet
- `sane-find-scanner -p` : probe parallel port scanners
- `sane-find-scanner -f` : force opening all explicitly given devices as SCSI and USB devices
