# ntfscmp

Compare two NTFS filesystems and tell the differences.

## Usage
- `ntfscmp [OPTIONS] DEVICE1 DEVICE2` : the general form, as the manual gives it
- `ntfscmp -P` : don't show progress bars
- `ntfscmp -v` : more informational output
- `ntfscmp -h` : display help and exit
