# linode-cli account

Manage Linode accounts.

## Usage
- `linode-cli account view` : view account
- `linode-cli account settings` : view account settings
- `linode-cli account payment-create --cvv cvv --usd amount_in_dollars` : make a payment
- `linode-cli account notifications-list` : view account notifications
