# ticketconverter.py

Convert Kerberos tickets between kirbi and ccache formats. Part of the Impacket suite.

## Usage
- `ticketConverter.py path/to/ticket.kirbi path/to/ticket.ccache` : convert kirbi to ccache
- `ticketConverter.py path/to/ticket.ccache path/to/ticket.kirbi` : convert ccache to kirbi
