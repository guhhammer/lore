# wikit

Easily get Wikipedia summaries.

## Usage
- `wikit topic` : show a short summary of a specific topic on Wikipedia
- `wikit topic -l language_code` : specify a language (ISO 639-1 language code)
- `wikit topic -b` : open the full Wikipedia article in the default browser
- `wikit topic -d` : open a disambiguation menu
